*Last updated: November 20, 2026*

Scroll Guilt is a free browser extension that reminds you to take short movement breaks when you open social media during your working hours.

## Data collection

This extension does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It does not use analytics, tracking pixels, or any third-party service.

## Local settings only

The extension lets you choose your active hours and how many seconds you must wait before dismissing a reminder. These preferences are stored **locally on your device**, using the browser's built-in `chrome.storage` API, and are never sent to any server.

## Permissions used

- **`storage`** — used only to save your preferences locally on your device.
- **Content scripts** on supported social media domains (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok) — used only to display an on-page reminder. No content from these pages is read, collected, or transmitted.

## Changes to this policy

Any future change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
